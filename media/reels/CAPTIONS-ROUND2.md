# Shelf Made reels, round 2: captions, hashtags, sound

Three more vertical reels (1080×1920, 30 fps, ~20 s, mastered to −14 LUFS), new content and a
new look built from the game's own UI: its font (Fredoka), its celebration banner, HUD pills,
review cards from its map listing, its confetti, and the yellow "Open shop" button.

Each comes in two versions:
- `ReelN….mp4`: royalty-free music baked in, every cut on its beat. Post as-is.
- `ReelN…-game-sound.mp4`: the same edit with only the game's own sounds (till beeps, cash,
  footsteps, birdsong). Add a trending song in Instagram's audio picker and keep the
  original sound at ~20–30 %.

Covers: `Cover4Day.jpg`, `Cover5PickYourShop.jpg`, `Cover6Reviews.jpg` (title in the middle
band, so the profile grid's crop keeps it).

Suggested order: **Reel 5 first** (pick one of six: the easiest comment there is), then Reel 6,
then Reel 4. One a day. Reply to every number in the comments ("good choice, that's the
Berry one!"), and pin a comment with the link line.

## Notes
- Every review text is word for word what the game generated (`reels/capture/reviews.cjs`
  plays days of a neglected shop and a well-run one and dumps them), and the photos on the
  cards are the reviewers' own in-game phone photos. The fixes shown are scripted takes of
  the real game.
- Trending lists: SocialPilot (Oct 12–25, 2026), SocialBee (Oct 14, 2026), Buffer. None gives
  BPMs or sections, so those are unverified. Business accounts get a narrower audio library.
- Copyrighted trending songs are never baked into the files: add them in the app.
- Making more: see `reels/README.md`.
